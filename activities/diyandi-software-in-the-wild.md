## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  

- Visitors attending Diyandi Festival in Iligan

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  

- While attending the Diyandi events, the visitors might find themselves in a situation where they accidentally left or lose their belongings. Because they are just visitors in Iligan City and aren't familiar with the area, they would have a hard time knowing who to go to when they attempt to report their lost items.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  

- The visitors attending the Diyandi Festival need a way to report their lost belongings and check if someone has found and turned them in. 

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  

- It might be difficult for them to approach those who are responsible of the task of dealing with the lost and found since they are not familair with the city.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  

- A mobile-friendly lost and found website where visitors can report their lost belongings and see if someone has handed them to the establishment. The website can also provide a single place where visitors can report their lost belongings without having to know which person or officer to approach.

**How would it help the intended users?**  

- The website would provide locations on where lost items can be reported or claimed and make it easier for visitors to deal with their problem.
---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. The users can submit a report about their lost belongings by providing a description of the item and where they last saw it.
2. The users can search through the category of found items to see if their lost items have been handed in.
---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: Easy to use

**Why does this matter to users?**  
- The website should have a simple and clear interface so visitors can quickly report or search for lost belongings without having a hard time navigating on it.

### Quality 2: Low data

**Why does this matter to users?**  

- From what I observed as a local resident in Iligan City, internet connection and data tends to be slow for safety reasons. Having that in mind, considering low data as one of its quality is matters so that users can still access it with limited or slower connection.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

- Ask visitors for feedback about whether they were able to report or found their lost belongings easily using the website. 
- Measure how long it took for the users to submit the report of their lost belongings.
