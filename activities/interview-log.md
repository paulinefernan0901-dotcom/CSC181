## Summary

Our team conducted in-person interviews with two stakeholder groups: undergraduate students of MSU-IIT and local sari-sari vendors around the campus. In total, we interviewed **six** people, **four students** and **two vendors**.

The interviews with Stakeholder Group 1, the undergraduate students, were all conducted on October 23, 2026. The four students are from BS Statistics (2nd Year, two students), BS Civil Engineering (3rd Year), and BS Mechanical Engineering (2nd Year), and all of them live in boarding houses near the campus. The interviews followed the daily essentials section, covering the items they buy, how often they buy them, their weekly budgets, the stores they visit, how far they are willing to walk for cheaper prices, and their biggest challenge in budgeting. Each interview lasted about 4 to 8 minutes. The students consented to having their photos taken, so photos from these interviews are included in this log.

The interviews with Stakeholder Group 2, the local vendors, were conducted on October 26, 2026. The two vendors run sari-sari stores and were interviewed on the same day. They were asked whether they would use an app that shows their item prices online, whether they would update prices manually, and how they would feel about their prices being compared to other stores. One vendor was also asked about the most bought items in their store. The vendors did not consent to having their photos or names taken, so they are listed as unnamed vendors (V1 and V2). Only audio recording was allowed, so the audio recordings are included in place of photos.
